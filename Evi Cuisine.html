What we offer

About Evi Cuisine

Evi Cuisine is a South-South-led restaurant built around seasonal produce, precise technique, and a guest experience that is as considered as the plate. From tasting menus to large-scale catering, every service is tracked, timed, and delivered with the same kitchen standard.

River dining

Lunch and dinner menus that change with the market: wood-fired proteins, composed vegetables, and desserts finished to order.

South tasting

A multi-course tasting built around the day’s best produce, with optional wine pairing and dietary accommodations on request.

Private events

Buyouts, celebrations, and corporate dinners in the dining room or chef’s table, with a dedicated service captain.

Catering & delivery

Packed platters, boxed lunches, and full-service catering with live order status from confirmation through drop-off.

Kitchen operations

Order tracking, in real time

Guests and hosts can follow every ticket from the pass to the door. These are the stages we measure — and the reliability we hold ourselves to.

  1. 1

    Order received

    Tickets land on the board the moment they are confirmed.

  2. 2

    Stop place

    Stations pull, portion, and fire according to ticket time.

  3. 3

    On the pass/Waka

    Quality check, garnish, and temperature before it leaves.

  4. 4

    Out for service

    Runner or courier updates until the guest is served.
